How it works. First the song is split into short hits (drums) and steady tones by looking at how each frequency changes over time: a drum hit is a short burst across many frequencies, a note is a steady line. From the steady tones, the parts that sound identical in both speakers become the vocal, since lead vocals are almost always mixed dead center. Low tones become bass, and the rest is other instruments. The four stems always add back up to the exact original. It works best on studio stereo mixes; mono files, live recordings and vocals with heavy stereo reverb separate less cleanly.
